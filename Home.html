What is the purpose of Spot play?

  • Spot play aims to introduce a platform that Ethiopian local artists can get listeners without much competition from more established artists abroad.

  • Spot play aims to popularize the music streaming industry in Ethiopia.

  • Spot play aims to benefit artists in the music industry of Ethiopian origin by providing higher royality checks while simultaneously earning profit through the services it offers to listeners.
