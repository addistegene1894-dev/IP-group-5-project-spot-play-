# Spot Play – Music Made Simple

**Discover. Listen. Enjoy. Your Music, Your Choice.**

Welcome to **Spot Play**, a music streaming website designed to make discovering and enjoying music simple, affordable, and accessible to everyone.

Inspired by modern music streaming platforms, Spot Play brings its own approach to music access by offering flexible listening options, affordable packages, and subscription plans that suit different users and budgets.

This project is being developed as a group frontend development project for the **Internet Programming** course at **Addis Ababa Science and Technology University (AASTU)**, using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

---

## About the Project

Music should be something everyone can enjoy without worrying about expensive subscriptions or complicated payment systems.

**That's the idea behind Spot Play.**

Users will be able to explore music, search for their favorite songs, create playlists, and choose how they want to access their music. Whether someone prefers listening for free, purchasing a music package, leasing individual songs, or subscribing to a premium plan, Spot Play aims to provide a flexible experience.

Our goal is to create an attractive, user-friendly, and interactive website that demonstrates how a modern music streaming platform could work.

---

## Key Features

The website will include several features inspired by the Spot Play system:

- **Music Discovery**: Explore available songs and find music based on your interests.
- **Search**: Easily search for songs, artists, and other music.
- **Free Listening**: Enjoy up to 30 music records (songs) every month without payment.
- **Music Packages**: Choose packages containing a specific number of songs at affordable prices.
- **Song Leasing**: Access individual songs or playlists for a selected period, such as a day, week, or month.
- **Personal Playlists**: Create, organize, and customize playlists with your favorite songs.
- **Subscription Plans**: Explore daily, weekly, and monthly subscription options.
- **Premium Experience**: Discover premium features, including playlist downloads and sharing.
- **Payment Information**: Explore the payment process and the available payment options in Ethiopian Birr (ETB).

## Technologies

We will use the following technologies to build the frontend:

- **HTML5** – To structure the website and organize its content.
- **CSS3** – To design the interface, create responsive layouts, and make the website visually appealing.
- **JavaScript** – To add interactivity and make the website more dynamic.

**Note:** No backend framework or database is part of this project's current scope. Our focus is on developing the frontend experience.

## Project Scope

**Spot Play is currently a frontend-only project.**

Our implementation will focus on the visual design, page structure, navigation, and interactive features of the website.

Features such as real payment processing, actual user authentication, database management, and backend music streaming services are **outside the scope** of this implementation.

Some features may therefore be demonstrated through sample data and simulated frontend interactions.

